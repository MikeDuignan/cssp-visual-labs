CSSP Visual Labs

Two interactive pages for COMP09031 Cybersecurity and Secure Programming (ATU Galway). They run in your browser; there is nothing to download or install.

Cookie Theft

Session hijacking, shown step by step.

  • A browser logs in and the server issues a session cookie
  • Every request carries the cookie
  • An attacker on the wire copies it and replays it
  • HTTPS as the defence

Password Check: Argon2id

How a server stores and checks a password.

  • A fresh random salt, then a memory-hard hash
  • Only the verifier is stored
  • Login: derive again and compare
  • Why an offline attacker is slowed down

Both pages work best on a screen at least 1280 by 720 pixels. All sites, accounts and data in them are fictional. They are for teaching only.
